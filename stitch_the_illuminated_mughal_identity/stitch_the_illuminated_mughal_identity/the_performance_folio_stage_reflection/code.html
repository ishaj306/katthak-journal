Vasant Utsav: The Spring Recital

Lucknow Bhavan March 14, 2024 Classical Solo

Performance Folio

Costume & Shringar

For this spring recital, the ensemble was inspired by the flora of the royal gardens. The Angarkha was crafted from hand-loomed Banarasi silk in a shade of dried pomegranate, paired with a sheer dupatta featuring 'Mukaish' silver-dot work. The makeup focused on the 'Nayika' aesthetic—defined kohl eyes and a deep vermillion bindi.

Post-Performance Reflections

The 'Tatkar' in Drut laya felt exceptionally grounded today. The connection with the Tabla player was intuitive; we found the 'Sam' perfectly during the complex Tihayis.
Lost balance briefly during the third 'Chakkar' of the Gat Nikas. I suspect the floor surface was slightly more polished than the rehearsal space.
The 'Abhinaya' segment for the Thumri requires more subtle eye movements. I felt I was over-empathizing with the face rather than let the internal emotion guide the sight.
Practice 108 Chakkars daily to stabilize the core. Begin memorizing the new 'Kavitt' from the Raigarh manuscript for the next season.

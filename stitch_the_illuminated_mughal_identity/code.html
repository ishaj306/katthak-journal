Vilambit Laya Tukda I

Date Recorded

14 Dec 2024

Guru

Pt. Birju Maharaj

Gharana

Lucknow

Taal

Teental (16 Beats)

Bols

Dha Kita Taka Dhum Kita Taka
Tage Tita Kita Taka Gadigana
Dha - Kita Taka - Dha -
Tage Tita Kita Taka - Dha
Thei Thei Tat, Aat Thei Thei Tat, Ta-Thei-Thei-Tat!

auto_stories Meaning & Interpretation

This Tukda, composed in the graceful Vilambit Laya (slow tempo), embodies the essential fluid movements of the Lucknow Gharana. The initial 'Dha Kita Taka' sequence mimics the resonant strikes of the Tabla, while the concluding 'Thei' emphasizes the vertical poise of the dancer. It is interpreted as a conversation between the feet and the floor, where every strike is a syllable of devotion.

directions_run Performance Instructions

  • 01. The first 'Dha' must be executed with a soft knee-bend, eyes following the right hand's trajectory.
  • 02. Maintain a 1-second pause during the 'Kita' to emphasize the rhythmic complexity.
  • 03. The final Chakkar (spin) should conclude exactly on the Sam, with the arms held in a 'Lotus' mudra.
